NAR

A critical examination of the New Apostolic Reformation's theology and practice.

This site exists to lay out, plainly and with sources, where the New Apostolic Reformation gets its theology wrong — drawing on roughly 25 years of firsthand experience inside the wider charismatic movement, not just outside observation.

This is not a neutral encyclopedia entry. It's a critique. Where a claim is a personal observation, it's labeled as one. Where it's a doctrinal or historical claim, it's sourced. The two are never presented as the same kind of evidence — but neither is this site pretending to have no position.
