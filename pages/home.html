Firsthand

Articles written from direct, lived experience — not secondhand summary.

This site collects long-form articles grounded in roughly 25 years of firsthand experience with the charismatic movement — inside it, close to it, watching it change. The goal isn't to score points for or against anyone. It's to write down what direct experience actually looked like, as clearly and honestly as possible, and to put it next to the historical and documented record so readers can judge for themselves.
