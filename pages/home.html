NAR

A critical examination of the New Apostolic Reformation's theology and practice.

This site exists to lay out, plainly and with sources, where the New Apostolic Reformation gets its theology wrong — drawing on roughly 25 years of firsthand experience inside the wider charismatic movement, not just outside observation. It's a critique, not a neutral encyclopedia entry — see the Introduction for the full explanation of that.
