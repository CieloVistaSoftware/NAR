Why this site exists

The premise

Most public writing about religious movements comes from one of two places: an insider defending it, or an outsider critiquing it from the outside looking in. Both have their place, but neither replaces someone who was actually inside for a long time and is now trying to describe it plainly.

This site is that attempt — articles written from roughly 25 years of direct, firsthand experience with the charismatic movement. Where a claim isn't firsthand, it's sourced. Where it's a personal observation, it's labeled as one. The two are never presented as the same kind of evidence.

What this site is not

It's not a debunking site, and it's not an apologetics site. It doesn't exist to tell anyone what to believe. It exists to put a clear, honest, searchable record in one place — the kind that's hard to find when most accounts are written by people who were never actually there.

Feedback and corrections

If something here is factually wrong, or a source is misrepresented, that matters more than being agreed with. Corrections are always welcome.
