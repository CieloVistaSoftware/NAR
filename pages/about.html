Why this site exists

The premise

This site takes a clear position: the New Apostolic Reformation's central theological claims — that the offices of apostle and prophet continue today with governing authority, and that Christians are mandated to seek control over civil society's institutions — are wrong, and worth explaining why in detail rather than just asserting.

That position is informed by roughly 25 years of direct, firsthand experience with the wider charismatic movement — not a critique written from complete outside distance. Where a claim isn't firsthand, it's sourced. Where it's a personal observation, it's labeled as one.

What this site is not

It's not a claim that every charismatic Christian, or every person who has ever attended a NAR-adjacent church, is acting in bad faith. Critique of a theological framework is not the same thing as an attack on the people who hold it. The goal is to examine the ideas — their biblical basis, their history, and their consequences — honestly enough that someone who disagrees would still recognize their own position as fairly stated before it's argued against.

Feedback and corrections

If something here misrepresents what NAR teaches, misquotes a source, or gets a fact wrong, that matters more than being agreed with. Corrections are always welcome — file them as an issue.
