## Televangelism precedents (Rex Humbard & Oral Roberts)

**Rex Humbard** — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rex_Humbard),
corroborated by [Christian Century's obituary](https://www.christiancentury.org/article/2007-10/rex-humbard-pioneer-tv-evangelist-dies);
the 1970s securities case is documented contemporaneously in
[Christianity Today, "Rex Humbard: 'We Didn't Go Under,'" 1976](https://www.christianitytoday.com/1976/01/rex-humbard-we-didnt-go-under/).

**Oral Roberts** — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oral_Roberts) and
[Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Oral-Roberts) for biography; the
1987 fundraising appeal and City of Faith's 1989 closure are corroborated by
[CNN's obituary coverage](https://www.cnn.com/2009/US/12/15/oral.roberts/index.html).
His authorship of the foreword to Bill Hamon's *Prophets and Personal Prophecy* —
the direct documented link to NAR's prophetic-apostolic lineage — is confirmed via
bibliographic/retail listing
([Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Prophets-Personal-Prophecy-Oral-Roberts/dp/0768432618)),
though the foreword's actual text wasn't independently read. A recent academic
biography, reviewed at
[Word&Way](https://wordandway.org/2023/06/27/review-oral-roberts-and-the-rise-of-the-prosperity-gospel/),
cautions against calling Roberts the sole "father" of the prosperity gospel — he's
better described as among its first major popularizers.

## Video

This is the weakest-sourced category, and that's stated plainly rather than papered
over. The January 6, 2021 shofar-blowing and prophetic-rhetoric footage that
journalists (including Rolling Stone's reporting above) have referenced was, as best
as could be confirmed, provided to reporters privately by researchers rather than
posted at one stable public URL. A video exists on the Internet Archive
(["Blowing shofar and putting up Cross message of Jesus at Capitol"](https://archive.org/details/44pTivX5qsChW8x35),
uploaded January 8, 2021) — it is real and live, but it's self-described by its
uploader as part of a pro-Trump "documentary... exposing the fraud," i.e. a partisan
participant's own footage, not something independently verified or specifically
cited by name in the journalism above. Treat it as illustrative at best, not as a
primary source.

For official ministry video (not journalism), Bethel Church's own YouTube channel is
linked directly from [bethel.com](https://www.bethel.com/), and Generals International
runs its own platform at [tv.generals.org](https://tv.generals.org/) — both are the
subjects' own self-presentation, not independent reporting, and are cited here as
such.
