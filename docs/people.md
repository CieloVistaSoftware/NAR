# People & Organizations

Every link below was checked before publishing, not guessed from memory. Where no
reliable link exists, that's stated explicitly rather than pointing somewhere
uncertain.

## New Apostolic Reformation figures

**C. Peter Wagner** (1930–2016) — coined "New Apostolic Reformation." No personal
site survives him. His institutional legacy continues as
[Wagner University](https://wagner.university/) (formerly Wagner Leadership
Institute), now chancellored by Ché Ahn — this is a successor institution, not
Wagner's own site.

**Cindy Jacobs** — co-founder of [Generals International](https://www.generals.org/),
alongside Mike Jacobs. Generals International also runs its own streaming platform,
[tv.generals.org](https://tv.generals.org/).

**Che Ahn** — founder of [Harvest International Ministry](https://www.harvestim.org/)
and chancellor of Wagner University. Personal site: [cheahn.org](https://www.cheahn.org/about/).

**Lou Engle** — founder of TheCall (the standalone organization wound down in 2018).
Current site, which discusses TheCall's history directly: [louengle.com](https://louengle.com/about/).

**Bill Johnson** — senior leader of Bethel Church, Redding, California. Official bio:
[bethel.com/people/bill-johnson](https://www.bethel.com/people/bill-johnson).

## Shepherding (Discipleship) movement figures

**Derek Prince** (1915–2003) — official ministry site, still active:
[derekprince.com](https://www.derekprince.com/).

**Bob Mumford** — [lifechangers.org](https://lifechangers.org/about-us/), his ministry
since 1973.

**Charles Simpson** — ministry continues as Charles Simpson Ministries / CSM
Publishing, now led by his son: [csmpublishing.org](https://csmpublishing.org/).

**Don Basham** (d. 1989) and **Ern Baxter** (d. 1993) — no official or successor sites
found for either. Both are documented in secondary sources on the Shepherding
movement rather than through any surviving site of their own.

## Critics and researchers

**Douglas Geivett** — Professor Emeritus, Biola University; co-author of the
cessationist critique cited in the [main article](?page=nar). Faculty profile:
[biola.edu/directory/people/douglas-geivett](https://www.biola.edu/directory/people/douglas-geivett).

**Holly Pivec** — co-author with Geivett. [hollypivec.com](https://www.hollypivec.com/about).

**Matthew D. Taylor** — religion scholar, Institute for Christian & Jewish Studies
(ICJS), author of the research connecting specific NAR apostolic networks to
January 6, 2021. [icjs.org/the-violent-take-it-by-force](https://icjs.org/the-violent-take-it-by-force/).
Note: searching his name alone can surface an unrelated UK political strategist of
the same name — the ICJS link above is the correct one.

publishes their statement of core beliefs and values directly.

**Generals International** — [generals.org](https://www.generals.org/). No standalone

media-focused rather than doctrine-focused.
